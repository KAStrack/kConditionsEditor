# Notes — 0.0.27

The add-button preview from 0.0.16 was described as "too messy". Ben asked for
something more subtle, and asked that `AND(…)` / `OR(…)` include the
operators that will go inside the brackets. Asked for options, he chose to keep
all of them. The look became an option of its own, `previewStyle`, combined
with the skin the way `theme` is.

## What was messy

Screenshots of every button's preview, on `ben AND FL AND (high OR urgent)`,
showed three things:

1. **Too many shapes.** `.kce-will-group` put a fill and an outline on every
   element in the set, separately. Each row is as wide as its content, so four
   rows were four ragged boxes stacked edge to edge — the "reads like a form"
   look the prose skin argues against. The group's tool strip carries
   `data-node`, so it got a box too.
2. **Holes where the operators are.** Connectors carry `data-conn-group` (they
   belong to a group, not a node), and `subtreeTargets()` collects
   `[data-node]`. The `AND` / `OR` between enclosed rows was never in the set.
   That was the reported bug.
3. **A ghost that shouts.** A filled dashed pill on a line of its own.

## The design: one set, seven renderings

Design idea 4 applied again. If each style decided what it marks, seven
styles would disagree within a release. So:

- `previewTargets(nodes, group)` is the only thing that decides. It returns
  the nodes' elements minus `.kce-group-tools`. It adds the connector *buttons*
  inside any enclosed group (prefix test on `data-conn-group`), and those of
  `group` that sit between two enclosed siblings. That last test needs
  `data-conn-index` on each connector, now set by `renderConnector()`. Buttons,
  not wrappers: in the tree layout the wrapper also holds the add buttons,
  including the one under the pointer.
- The hue class (`is-and`, `is-or`, `kce-will-remove`) sets `--kce-mark`,
  `--kce-mark-soft` and `--kce-mark-edge`. Every style paints with those.
  They map onto tokens that already exist in all four palette places, so no
  new palette colour was needed.
- `paintPreview()` adds the classes. For `fade` it sets `kce-previewing` on
  the tree; for `bar` and `region` it calls `drawPreviewMark()`.

| style | how |
|---|---|
| `highlight` | the old rules, scoped to `[data-kce-preview="highlight"]` |
| `bar` | one absolutely positioned element, 3px wide, beside the union of the set's rects |
| `region` | the same element, sized to the union, filled at 12% of the hue |
| `fade` | unmarked rows, brackets and operators to 0.3 opacity |
| `color` | labels, values, brackets and operators in the set to `--kce-mark` |
| `marker` | the grip's glyph replaced by `●` (`--kce-preview-marker`); a row without a grip gets the dot in its margin |
| `underline` | field and operator names and AND / OR underlined in `--kce-mark` |

The ghost is written as rule text in every style but `highlight`: the joining
operator in its hue, then *new condition* in muted italics.

### Decisions worth recording

- **Default `bar`.** Ben's choice, over keeping `highlight`, with pickers on
  the demo and skins pages so the rest can be compared.
- **× follows the option.** One setting governs every preview.
- **`region` paints over the text, not under it.** Under (with `z-index: -1`
  in an isolated tree) disappeared behind any skin that gives rows a
  background — boxed's cards. At 12% it tints rather than obscures.
- **A shared-line bracket does not set the extent.** In prose a group's `(`
  sits on the previous sibling's line. Measured, it stretched the bar and
  region over a row the button does not touch. It stays marked; it just does
  not count toward the shape.
- **Named `previewStyle`, not `hoverStyle`.** It fires on keyboard focus too.

### The invariant kept

No style may move anything. The hovered button has the pointer, and one that
moved would lose it, clear the preview, move back and flicker — the 0.0.16
lesson. Overlays and the marker dot are absolutely positioned; everything
else is colour, opacity or decoration; the ghost still goes after the button's
own line. `preview-styles.js` asserts the button's rect is unchanged in every
style, skin and case.

## Testing

`tests/browser/preview-styles.js` covers 7 styles × 3 skins × 5 cases
(`OR(…)` on the rule, `AND(…)` on a nested group, `+AND` on an OR row, `+AND`
on the rule, × on a nested group), with 872 checks. Each is judged by what is
painted: computed colour, text decoration, the opacity chain, or containment
in the overlay's rect.

Two judging details matter:

- **"Left alone" is compared with the element's own look before the hover.**
  An outside `AND` that is blue anyway is not evidence of anything.
- **Boxed draws no operator words**, so its operator checks are skipped rather
  than passed vacuously.

Lesson 6 was checked: with the connector half of `previewTargets()` disabled,
the script fails 29 checks, all of them "operators inside the range are
marked".

The four older scripts that assert the highlight look (`add-preview`,
`removal-preview`, `removal-preview-colours`, `dist-bundles`) are pinned to
`previewStyle: 'highlight'`, because that is what they test.

## Found on the way

In this sandbox `grep` is a ugrep wrapper. It prints paths without `./` and
skips git-ignored directories, so CLAUDE.md's version-bump exclusions
(`^./dist/` and the rest) did not match. The bump rewrote the historical
headings in `docs/notes/`, which then had to be restored by hand. Ben's shell is
unaffected; in the sandbox, use `command grep`.
