# Notes — 0.0.29

Two requests from Ben, from KAStrack:

> Is there currently a configurable option that determines whether the
> operator buttons appear on hover, or are always visible? If not, let's add
> that option. Also the move arrow, x icon and and/or button that should be to
> the right of a row sometimes get wrapped to be below it, depending on the
> length of the field, operator, and input/select in the value … Can we ensure
> they are always on the same line, even if it means we have to shorten
> something else?

## What was actually wrapping

A sweep across rows of six shapes, three skins and six widths (380–900px)
found two causes, neither of them the row's own controls. 0.0.26 had already
made `.kce-condition` `nowrap`.

1. **The prose line.** A `.kce-line` holds the row, its AND / OR and its add
   buttons, and it was `flex-wrap: wrap`. Whenever the row filled the line,
   the AND / OR and the buttons wrapped onto a line of their own. At 545px that
   was nearly every row; at 760px, any row with a long field name.
2. **Multi-part values.** `.kce-values` wrapped, so `between`, a date range or
   a tag list could take two or more lines. The row centred everything on
   that block, which put the arrows and × halfway down it, below the field
   name.

## What gives way, in order

Ben accepted shortening something else. The order is:

1. **The value**, down to a floor kept by the value slot and chosen by what it
   holds: 60px for text, 8em for a date or a tag list, 8em for two values,
   16em for two dates. The shrink weight is 100000, not 100. At 100, a field
   name took a ~1px share of the squeeze and showed an ellipsis while the
   value still had 50px to give.
2. **The field and operator names**, down to `min(natural, 2.5em)`, which
   `fitRows()` measures. A floor in the stylesheet would pad a short name like
   "is" out to 2.5em.
3. **The line scrolls sideways.** If the row still cannot fit, `fitRows()`
   gives it its full width, and the line overflows into the tree's horizontal
   scroll. Before, the row's content spilled out of its own box, over its own
   AND / OR. In practice this happens below about 480px.

The floors live on the slot, not on the inputs. Floors on the inputs let them
spill out of a slot squeezed below their sum, over the arrows.

Rows and lines align on baselines, not centres. A tag list may still wrap
(it has to), but it grows downwards, with the field name, controls and
AND / OR on its first line.

## The add buttons: reserved, then floated

The AND / OR now never leaves the line, so the add buttons after it had to
get their room somewhere. Ben was offered three ways and chose to reserve
their room at rest (invisible), so that pointing at a line moves nothing. I
estimated the cost at about 45px per row and 130px on a group's last row.

Built and measured, it was 66px and **184px**. In a 545px dialog the group's
last row was pushed to every floor: "As… is …", chips one per line, beside a
wide blank area. Shown the screenshots, Ben chose to float them instead.

- **`'always'`:** in the line, visible, keeping their room. A fair cost, since
  the buttons are on screen.
- **`'hover'`, prose:** `floatAdds()` marks every strip that ends its line;
  `positionAdds()` places it after the AND / OR if the line has space,
  otherwise as a strip just below it (`kce-adds-below`), with a ground and
  shadow of its own. `pointer-events: none` until its line is hovered or
  focused, so an invisible strip never takes the pointer from the line under
  it. It stays in the tab order.
- A strip followed by a bracket (the row before a group) stays in the flow,
  or it would sit on the bracket.
- The tree skins are unchanged: their add buttons sit on a connector line of
  their own, where growing moves nothing.

A prose group's tools got the same treatment. In the flow, on a line that
cannot wrap, their 136px would come out of the row before the bracket. They
float after the `(` (`kce-tools-float`), and still go to the `)` line when
they don't fit.

`alignConnectors()`'s guard changed with the layout. A push used to be
refused when it grew the line, which was a wrap. Now it is refused when it
narrows the row or overflows the line.

## `controls`

`'hover'` (default) or `'always'`. It covers the add buttons, as Ben asked,
and also the grip, arrows, × and group tools: one option for all of them, his
choice. `applyControls()` writes `data-kce-controls`, and forces `'always'`
when `matchMedia('(hover: none)')` matches, kept live because a tablet can
gain a mouse. That replaced the `(hover: none)` media blocks in both
stylesheets, which had to repeat every reveal rule. Now a new hover-revealed
control needs one attribute rule.

## Testing

`tests/browser/one-line-rows.js` covers 3 skins × 2 modes × 3 widths × 6 row
shapes, with every row hovered for real (1773 checks):

- the arrows, ×, AND / OR and the value's first line are on the field name's
  line;
- nothing spills out of a row or its value slot;
- names keep their floor;
- the value input does not move between rest and hover;
- no sideways scroll at 545px and up (one documented exception: a two-date
  range in a nested row with `'always'`);
- hover-mode floats don't take the pointer at rest, and are painted and
  reachable on hover;
- the option, its setter and its fallback;
- a no-hover screen gets `'always'`.

Lesson 6, checked twice. With prose lines allowed to wrap again, 48 checks
fail. With rows centred again, no check failed at first: the field name moves
down with the controls, so they stay level with it. The check now includes
the value's first line, and centring fails the tag rows.

`narrow-host.js`'s "no prose line wraps" now ignores absolutely positioned
children: a float below its line is a float, not a wrap.

## Not done

- Below about 480px, a group's last row (or a nested date range) still scrolls
  sideways in `'always'` mode. The visible add buttons need the room, and the
  alternatives were unreadable names or an untypeable value.
